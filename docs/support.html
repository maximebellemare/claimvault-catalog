Subscriptions

ClaimVault subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel them at Apple subscription settings. In the app, open Settings → Restore purchases if your paid entitlement is missing.

Privacy and document issues

Do not share unredacted financial statements, bank identifiers, card numbers or sensitive personal data publicly. For non-sensitive technical issues or feature requests, open an issue. If you cannot use that channel, consult the app's App Store support contact once available.

Legal and financial accuracy

ClaimVault is not a bank or claim-filing service. Any potentially incorrect billing item requires verification with the provider. File legitimate settlement claims through official administrator websites.

ClaimVault is an independent transaction-review tool, not a bank, law firm or settlement administrator. No refunds are guaranteed.
