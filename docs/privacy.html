Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

ClaimVault is a mobile application for reviewing transaction information and independently published settlement listings. This policy explains the data handled by the app.

On-device statement analysis

When you select a bank or credit-card PDF, ClaimVault uses Apple's on-device document analysis to identify potential transaction dates, merchant names and amounts. The statement-history feature does not upload your statement PDF or store its raw extracted text. Only after you explicitly save history does it retain selected transaction dates, merchants, amounts, confirmed currency and an account nickname in local app storage. No bank login is required.

Evidence and recovery records

If you choose to save receipts, receipt photos, claim references, watched brands, manually entered billing details or recovery-case statuses, those records are stored locally on your device. The app does not independently verify whether a refund or settlement payment is owed. Your original source documents may remain in iOS Files or other apps.

Payments

For subscriptions, Apple processes purchases and RevenueCat processes subscription identifiers, entitlement and transaction status to provide premium access and purchase restoration. ClaimVault does not receive your complete card number. Apple determines eligibility for introductory offers and displays the applicable localized price and renewal terms before purchase. RevenueCat and Apple each operate under their own privacy policies.

Internet connections

ClaimVault may request publicly reviewed settlement catalogs using HTTPS. The hosting providers can process standard connection metadata, including IP addresses, to respond to those requests. Personal PDF statements are not sent to the settlement catalog.

Notifications, sharing and contacts

When you choose to set reminders, the app schedules local iOS notifications. When you initiate Share, iOS shows a share sheet for your review. Nothing is sent to a merchant, airline, court or settlement administrator without your own action.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved statement history and local records through ClaimVault's privacy controls. Files you exported, files in other apps, and device or cloud backups are not controlled by the app and may persist under their respective policies. iOS backup protection depends on device settings. The app does not claim a separate encryption layer for its local SQLite database.

Children and security

ClaimVault is not intended for children. Protect your iPhone with a passcode and do not enter full card numbers or sensitive login credentials in account nicknames or notes. Automated document recognition may be inaccurate: check results against original statements.

Questions and requests

Visit our support page for assistance. Do not post sensitive financial records in public issue trackers. We will update this page when handling practices change.

ClaimVault is an independent transaction-review tool, not a bank, law firm or settlement administrator. No refunds are guaranteed.
